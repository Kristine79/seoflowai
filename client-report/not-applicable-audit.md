   - Registration-based: Nextdoor, AngelList, Pinterest, GitHub, Twitter/X, SlideShare, ProductHunt (require account + verify)
   - Partner directories: HubSpot, Semrush, Webflow (have public agency directories with partner application)
   - Find Best SEO (status unknown, needs check)

2. **Alignable** is NOT in NOT_APPLICABLE — correct (it's NEEDS_MANUAL due to Business Identity Verification)

3. **Next steps recommended:**

   - Update human-queue.json
   - For NEEDS_MANUAL platforms: one can attempt human-assisted registration
   - For PAID platforms: discuss budget with client
